        Checking PC wake support…

        Keep Aerowave running, including in the tray. With wake for alarms on, the PC stays awake through an alarm and its snoozes. The display can turn off between rings. After final dismissal or automatic stopping without another snooze, Windows can sleep normally unless another alarm or activity needs it awake. Each snooze also requests a wake timer in case you put the PC to sleep yourself. Waking requires compatible hardware and Windows wake timers to be allowed. Modern Standby wake is unverified; keep the PC awake for dependable alarms on those systems. Alarms cannot wake a shut-down PC.
