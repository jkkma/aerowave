        Alarms & sleep

        Checking PC wake support…

        How waking works

        Keep Aerowave running, including in the tray. The PC stays awake through an alarm and its snoozes; the display can turn off between rings. Each snooze also requests a wake timer if you put the PC to sleep yourself.

        After the final dismissal or automatic stop, Windows can sleep normally unless another alarm or activity needs it awake.

        Waking needs compatible hardware and Windows wake timers enabled. Modern Standby wake is unverified; keep those PCs awake for dependable alarms. Alarms cannot wake a shut-down PC.

        Screen wake and quick controls

        A ringing alarm shows Dismiss and Snooze over the lock screen. Shake firmly several times to snooze, or press either volume button while the alarm screen is open to dismiss. Test alarms can only be dismissed.

        Full-screen access alone does not guarantee that every phone will wake its display. Schedule an alarm with the screen off to check it.

        Alarms after a restart

        Keep the phone powered on. After restarting, music folders may need the first unlock; the system alarm sound is the fallback. Force-stopping Aerowave prevents alarms until you open it again.
